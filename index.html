Aliens Land In The School Parking Lot

Eye Witness Accounts

According to several witnesses, a group of aliens landed in the school parking lot at approximately 3:00 PM on Tuesday. The witnesses reported seeing a bright light and hearing a loud noise before the aliens appeared. Fortunately, the aliens did not appear ot be hostile and did not cause any harm to the students. Apparantely, the aliens left about 10 minutes after landing, and no one was able to communicate with them. The school administration has stated that they will be investigating the incident further and will provide updates as more information becomes available.

Official Government Accounts

The local government has confirmed that they are aware of the incident and are currently investigating. They have stated that they will be releasing a statement to the public once they have more information. The government has also urged anyone who may have witnessed the incident to come forward and provide any information they may have.

Reported by Samantha Smith with MMCC News

We thank Samantha Smith for her reporting on this incident.
